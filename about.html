Professional Bio & Research Focus

I am a systems developer and researcher based at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee). My work focuses on building resilient autonomous software architectures, real-time robotics telemetry, and high-performance web platforms for academic and industrial research.

Currently, I serve as a Research Intern at IIT Roorkee under the direct supervision of Dr. Neetesh Kumar (Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science & Engineering), a tenure that began on July 6, 2026 (ongoing). In this capacity, my efforts are dedicated to two active research initiatives: Autonomous Drone Development and Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA).

Our lab solves the critical problem of GPS-denied UAV navigation. We formulated a multi-stage localization pipeline leveraging mono-camera trigonometric triangulation, ArUco fiducials, and YOLOPoint deep keypoint extraction, subsequently extended with an Intel RealSense D455 active stereo depth camera for dense spatial point-cloud mapping.

In parallel, I am actively investigating Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) tailored for decentralized edge networks and autonomous swarms (aligned with NIST SP 800-207). My research formulates lightweight Policy Decision and Enforcement Points (PDP/PEP), cryptographic mutual authentication (mTLS/SPIFFE), and telemetry-driven dynamic risk assessment to enforce least-privilege security across untrusted edge environments without degrading sub-24ms platform loop latencies.

Beyond robotics and security research, I architect production digital platforms — from international symposium registration systems with automated Razorpay webhooks and AI ID validation, to official faculty research portals and quantitative financial forecasting pipelines.
