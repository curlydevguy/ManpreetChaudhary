Stage 01 // Visual Markers

ArUco Fiducial Tracking & Marker Pose

Sub-pixel corner detection and 6-DOF relative pose estimation relative to structured landing platforms and known waypoints, providing absolute ground-truth orientation.

Fiducial: ArUco Dict 6x6 (250)
Corner Sub-Pixel: cv2.cornerSubPix
Solver: solvePnP (Iterative)
ArUco OpenCV Python
Stage 02 // Triangulation

Mono-Camera Epipolar Triangulation

Mathematical reconstruction of 3D Euclidean coordinates across continuous video frames from a single monocular lens, utilizing trigonometric parallax, intrinsic camera calibration matrices, and fundamental matrix estimation.

Geometry: P_c = K [R | t] P_w
Calibration: Radial Distortion Fix
Drift: Closed-Loop Corrected
Epipolar Geometry Triangulation C++
Stage 03 // Deep Features

YOLOPoint Neural Keypoint Extraction

Real-time embedded neural network inference generating dense, illumination-invariant visual keypoints and descriptors for robust visual odometry across dynamic, unstructured, and low-contrast environments.

Architecture: YOLOPoint CNN
Inference: Edge TensorRT / ONNX
Robustness: Motion Blur Invariant
YOLOPoint Deep Learning Edge AI
Stage 04 // Depth Fusion

Active Intel RealSense D455 Fusion

Extended spatial pipeline utilizing the Intel RealSense D455 active infrared stereo camera, generating dense 3D point-clouds and metric depth maps up to 6 meters for real-time obstacle avoidance and spatial clearance.

Sensor: Intel RealSense D455
Field of View: 86° × 57° Global Shutter
Loop Latency: < 24ms Response
RealSense D455 3D Point Clouds ROS2
